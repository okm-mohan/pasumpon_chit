# Pasumpon Community Chit Management System – Module Overview

## Introduction

**Pasumpon** is a community-based financial management system designed to manage the savings and lending activities of approximately **400 community members**. The system consists of four primary financial modules along with accounting, reporting, and analytics features.

---

# 1. Pandu (Monthly Savings Scheme)

The **Pandu** module is a monthly savings scheme that encourages members to save a fixed amount every month.

### Scheme Details

* Scheme Duration: **12 Months**
* Monthly Contribution per Pandu: **₹100**
* A member may subscribe to one or multiple Pandu schemes.

**Example:**

* 4 Pandu subscriptions = ₹400 monthly contribution
* Total contribution after 12 months:

  * ₹400 × 12 = ₹4,800
* On successful completion of the scheme, the member receives:

  * Total Savings + One Additional Month Contribution
  * ₹4,800 + ₹400 = **₹5,200**

### Features

* Separate **Pandu Collection Screen**
* Easy member selection
* Monthly due entry
* View active Pandu subscriptions for each member
* Automatic calculation of payable and receivable amounts
* Due date management

### Payment Rules

* Monthly dues must be paid **on or before the 10th of every month**.
* If a member misses a payment, they may pay the pending amount in the following month.
* The system should support:

  * Full payment of outstanding dues
  * Partial payment of outstanding dues
* Outstanding balances must be tracked automatically.

### Reports

* Member-wise Pandu Report
* Collection Report
* Pending Due Report
* Monthly Collection Summary
* Completed Pandu Report
* Outstanding Balance Report

---

# 2. Kanthu (Short-Term Loan)

The **Kanthu** module is a short-term lending system where the company provides loans to members with interest.

### Loan Rules

* Loans are issued with a predefined interest rate.
* At the time of loan disbursement, the **first month's interest is deducted** before releasing the amount to the member.

Example:

Loan Amount: ₹10,000

First Month Interest: ₹500

Amount Paid to Member: ₹9,500

### Repayment Rules

* Members can repay the loan balance at any time during the current financial year.
* If the member settles the loan within **four months**, no additional interest is charged after the first month's deduction.
* If the loan remains unpaid after four months, the system should automatically calculate and apply interest on the outstanding balance according to the configured interest rate.

### Features

* Loan Disbursement
* Interest Calculation
* Balance Tracking
* Partial Repayment
* Full Settlement
* Outstanding Loan Management
* Interest History

### Reports

* Loan Register
* Outstanding Loans
* Interest Collection Report
* Member Loan History
* Settlement Report

---

# 3. Ayul Santha (Long-Term Loan)

The **Ayul Santha** module is a long-term lending scheme.

Unlike Kanthu, members are **not required to repay the principal within a fixed period**.

### Loan Rules

* Members pay **only the monthly interest** on the outstanding principal amount.
* The principal amount may be repaid:

  * Fully
  * Partially
  * At any time, based on the member's convenience.
* Interest should always be calculated based on the current outstanding principal.

### Features

* Long-Term Loan Management
* Monthly Interest Collection
* Partial Principal Repayment
* Full Principal Settlement
* Outstanding Balance Calculation
* Interest Tracking

### Reports

* Monthly Interest Collection
* Outstanding Principal Report
* Member Loan Statement
* Loan History
* Interest Ledger

---

# 4. Accounts (Income & Expenses)

The system should include a complete accounting module similar to **Tally**, enabling the company to manage all financial transactions.

### Expense Management

* Office Expenses
* Salary
* Rent
* Electricity
* Maintenance
* Miscellaneous Expenses
* Cash Payments

### Income Management

Income should automatically include:

* Pandu Profit
* Kanthu Interest Income
* Ayul Santha Interest Income

### Accounting Features

* Ledger Management
* Cash Book
* Income & Expense Statement
* Day Book
* Voucher Entry
* Trial Balance
* Profit & Loss Summary
* Account-wise Reports

When loans are issued (Kanthu or Ayul Santha), the disbursed amount should be recorded as a cash outflow. Interest income earned from these modules should automatically appear under Income.

# Dashboard Requirements

Each of the four modules should have its own dedicated dashboard.

The dashboard should display:

* Total Collections
* Total Outstanding Amount
* Monthly Collections
* Active Members
* Pending Payments
* Recent Transactions
* Monthly Income
* Monthly Expenses
* Basic Charts and Analytics
* Quick Access Shortcuts

Although each dashboard should focus on its respective module, the sidebar should provide access to all reports across the system.

The search should provide:

* Fast autocomplete search
* Search by Member Name
* Search by Member ID
* Search by Mobile Number
* Search by Village
* Keyboard-friendly navigation
* Easy-to-use dropdown selection
* Consistent design across all modules
